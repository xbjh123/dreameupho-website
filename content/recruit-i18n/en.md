What is DreamEupho?  
DreamEupho is a fan-made visual novel production team based on Sound! Euphonium.  
• Organizational model: Non-profit, online fan collaboration.  
• Team structure: The team is coordinated by a permanent core management team, with multiple specialized production units organized by member skills to drive production.  
• Production method: We employ a skill-based team management structure and an industrial pipeline modeled after professional studios. Through rigorous creative standards and a multi-tier review system, we address member turnover and ensure consistent output for the game project.  
Join DreamEupho  
Out of pure love for the original work, we are looking for like-minded creators to join our development pipeline.  
This document is updated regularly, and specific open positions will be adjusted dynamically as the project progresses. If you would like to contribute your skills or passion to this project, feel free to submit an application at any time!

Project Member Recruitment — Open Positions and Reference Requirements
