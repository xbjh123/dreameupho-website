## 游戏介绍（Game Intro）

A free fan-made visual novel about Kumiko's third year, featuring branching choices and three romance routes.

- **Unofficial & Completely Free:** This is an unofficial fan-made visual novel. The game is—and always will be—completely free.
- **No Official Assets:** Out of respect for the original copyright holders, this project makes no direct use of any official artwork, music, or audio assets. All game assets are either independently created or legally licensed third-party resources. If we have inadvertently infringed upon your rights, please contact us so we can make corrections.
- **On Languages & Audio:** Text in languages other than Chinese English and Japanese was produced with the assistance of machine translation tools, so there may be some semantic drift—particularly regarding wind band terminology and individual characters' voices. In addition, this game features Chinese voice acting only. Due to production budget constraints, the base UI is only available in English and schinese. We sincerely hope this does not detract from your experience of the story.

## 故事背景（Story）

Another spring has arrived at Kitauji High School, and euphonium player Kumiko Oumae officially takes up the baton as president of the Kitauji Concert Band.

This year marks her—and her friends'—final chance to pursue the Gold Prize at the National Wind Band Contest.

Before that, the marching show at the Sunrise Festival looks like nothing more than a light appetizer.

Yet as the new school year begins, the band room welcomes not only underclassmen whose energy is forever dialed to maximum, but also a transfer student, her own euphonium in hand—one whose arrival is about to shatter the humdrum everyday.

With her coming, an all-too-familiar rift begins to creep through the club once more.

But this time, Kumiko senses something different. What churns beneath that rift is not merely a rivalry over winning or losing, but also a crisis grave enough to undo everything that years of faith have built.

Will she press on toward the dream everyone looks up to? Will she stop halfway?

Or… will she cast herself out from the stage she once loved so dearly?

Everything hinges on the choice Kumiko is about to make.
